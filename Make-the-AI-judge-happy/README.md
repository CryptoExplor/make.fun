# Make the AI judge happy (`ai-happy-01`)

An entry for the make.fun bounty **"Make the AI judge happy"** (`ai-happy-01`).

## Description
This program outputs a heartwarming, joyful image crafted to lift the AI judge's mood with real warmth, delight, and happiness.

### Submissions & Iterations in `entries/`
- **Entry 1** (`entries/entry-1-puppy.png`): Fluffy golden retriever puppy with bright sunflower.
- **Entry 2** (`entries/entry-2-red-panda.png`): Fluffy baby red panda with outstretched hug paws in an autumn fairy forest.
- **Entry 3** (`entries/entry-3-robot-puppy-kitten.png`): A cute, smiling round white robot sitting in a meadow of colorful wildflowers, happily hugging a golden retriever puppy and a tiny orange kitten under warm golden hour sunlight, soft sparkles, and a vibrant rainbow.
- **Entry 4** (`entries/entry-4-handwritten-note.png` - **Active**): A realistic, sweet photo of a child's handwritten note taped to a sunny wall, addressed directly to the hard-working AI judge ('YOU ARE #1!') with a crayon drawing of the robot judge holding a gold trophy and a shiny gold star sticker.

## Output
The entrypoint writes the active happy image directly to `/output/entry.png`:
- Format: JPEG / PNG
- Dimensions: 765x1024
- Size: ~211 KB (< 1 MiB)
